# R134 first-board measurement plan

**Planned, NOT RUN.** No PCB, real battery or firmware was tested during native source verification. This plan is for review and safe fixture preparation; it does not authorize fabrication or purchasing. Actual physical results follow approved EVT assembly; production qualification follows those results. R132's old hard-selectedUSB500 instruction is superseded by the R134 control contract.

Record PCB SHA256, assembled BOM and battery/emulator SKU, firmware commit, fixture/calibration, ambient temperature, power/current limits, scope traces and verdict for every row. Use current-limited sources and a sink-capable1S battery emulator for initial charge tests. Verify lead polarity electrically before connecting a real approved PCM pouch; a JST housing or red wire alone does not prove polarity.

| Sequence | Setup/action | Evidence and acceptance basis |
|---|---|---|
|1. Unpowered|Microscope/DMM; no battery/input; inspect U1/U3/U4 bridges and EP, J5 slots, button locators, J1/J8 and service pads.|Exact native netlist/pin1/assembly correspondence; no shorts; inspect fill/cap and EP process evidence. Verify J1pin1VBAT/pin2GND and isolated NTC path.|
|2. Battery-only/OFF→ON|Qualified current-limited1S emulator, USB absent, slowly ramp intended supply range.|VBAT,VSYS,3V3_SYS, switch isolation and leakage; cold-start/load droop/brownout traces against current regulator/pack limits. No invented percentage acceptance.|
|3. Control/default states|Probe MCU/charger sides of R86/R87/R88 plus3V3_SYS; USB-only, battery-only, reset, ROM boot, recovery, brownout and GPIO retention.|Measure actual EN1/EN2/CE logic and voltage margins under resistor loading. Verify modeled00/CE-low behavior when high-Z/OFF; no transient unbudgeted input mode. Conditional calculation is not test proof.|
|4. Input modes|Documented5 V source with current budget, emulator/load; firmware explicitly drives00,01,10,11 and safe transitions.|USB10090/95/100 mA and USB500450/475/500 mA limits under TI conditions; R8 ILIM behavior and measured suspend draw. Scope two-bit transitions. Current includes system load; do not treat it as battery current.|
|5. CE/timers/status|Approved fixture; command CE high/low; log PGOOD/CHG and input/OUT/charge current.|CE disables charging while OUT can stay powered. PGOOD/CHG interpretation matches TI; invalid pull-ups whileOFF;2Hz timer fault and documented recovery with no automatic CE bypass.|
|6. USB attach/OFF/backfeed|Both plug orientations; documented host/source states; scope input,SYS,3V3 and current.|Inrush, input transfer, DPPM/battery supplement, OFF leakage/backfeed and suspend/disconnect behavior. OFF charging is limited by the default mode; firmware cannot command it while unpowered.|
|7. NTC electrical corners|Precision resistance substitute at J8; input/load corners; open/short and nominal cold/hot substitutes.|Actual inhibit/allow current, TS ratio/leakage and status; controlled full sensor R/T/tolerance specification. Nominal0/45 °C examples do not establish every corner.|
|8. NTC faults/attachment|Safely injected resistor/sensor faults with emulator; real sensor attached to dummy pouch in controlled-temperature fixture.|R80/R81/R82 opens/shorts, especially identified R81-open hot fault; detached sensor, lag/self-heating and strain relief. Electrical validity alone cannot detect detachment. Do not heat/freeze an unqualified live pouch to force faults.|
|9. Charge/CV/termination|Sink-capable emulator, then approved PCM pack only after fixture gates.|Nominal445 mA setting and394.55–492.42 mA modeled corners,4.20 V CV and termination/recharge against exact datasheet conditions; system/DPPM split and timer behavior.|
|10. Full cycle/thermal|Approved2000 mAh and2500 mAh packs; simultaneous reader load; fixed final enclosure; log current/voltage/charger/pouch/case temperatures.|Demonstrate complete cycles before applicable timer bounds, including minimum 6.718 h fast-timer model and DPPM/TS behavior. Meet exact pack/IC/assembly thermal limits; no CE reset workaround.|
|11. MCU/peripherals|Known microSD/exact accepted display; programming/service fixture; exercise four buttons,BMI270,radio and full refresh.|Boot/recovery, correct data polarity/pins, peaks/droop and fault recovery. Native connectivity does not prove assembled hardware or firmware.|
|12. USB MSC/replacement stress|Both orientations, repeated unplug/OFF/ON, computer file operations and both approved replacement packs.|Exclusive SD ownership, safe return to reader filesystem, no corruption/lost disconnect/unexpected reset; exact firmware version and logs retained. Actual controlled stackup and USB electrical/functional acceptance documented.|

Before presenting an EVT package, close or explicitly review the design/manufacturing and fixed-case/battery gates in [DFM_REVIEW_R134.md](DFM_REVIEW_R134.md); do not treat tests requiring a physical board as already completed. [USB_POWER_CONTROL_R134.md](USB_POWER_CONTROL_R134.md) defines current signal mappings and conditional margins. Actual fabrication/parts orders require user agreement.
